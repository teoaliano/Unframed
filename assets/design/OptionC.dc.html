Gains
Real iteration: change one word, run again. The canvas can say a result is out of date with its sources, which is the thing wires were carrying.
Costs
A live dependency is a wire whether or not it is drawn — deleting a source has to mean something, and the graph is back to having edges nobody placed. Hardest of the three to keep honest.
