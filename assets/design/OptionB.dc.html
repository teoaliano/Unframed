Gains
“Make another one of these” becomes one click on a box that is already named. Presets, multi‑run and a saved model all have an obvious home, and the sheet can open on a set‑up recipe rather than a default.
Costs
Two kinds of group, told apart by a chip — and settings living inside a container that used to be pure contents. A loose selection still needs Option A’s behaviour, so this is an addition, not a replacement.
